04

The redesign

Before, legacy console
  • One flat alert table, newest-first, no severity weighting
  • Map and camera tools in separate windows
  • Telemetry buried in a collapsible log drawer
  • Takeover required confirming through two modal dialogs
  • Auto-resolutions shown as a bare "✓ resolved" with no reasoning
After, Fleet
  • Severity-ranked, grouped triage queue with live countdowns
  • One click on a queue row flies the map and loads the feed
  • Camera, LiDAR and telemetry unified in a single context panel
  • Emergency takeover is a persistent, single-action control
  • Every auto-resolution carries a confidence chip + "why" rationale
